Features

Everything your team or community talks about, in one place.

Organized channels, real conversations and the tools to keep them tidy. Every screenshot on this page is the real app.

Threads keep busy channels readable

Reply in a thread from any message and the side conversation stays out of the main channel. Each thread has its own notifications, so you only follow the ones you care about.

  • Markdown and code blocks with syntax highlighting
  • Reactions, pins, mentions and a recent threads menu
  • Sections to group channels the way your team works

Voice and video, built into every server

Drop into a voice channel to talk, turn your camera on, or share your screen with its audio. Everyone in the server can see who's in which call.

  • Calls go through a relay, so participants never see each other's IP address
  • Noise suppression, speaking indicators and a full-screen stage
  • A soundboard for the moments that need an airhorn

Search that finds the message, not just the channel

Full-text search across a whole server and your DMs. Narrow it down by person, channel or date, and jump straight to the message in context.

  • from:, in:, before: and after: filters
  • Matches highlighted across every channel at once

Share files, images and links

Drag and drop or paste to upload. Images show inline, audio and video play right in the chat, and link previews are fetched by the server so a stranger's link never learns your IP.

  • Custom server emoji and GIF search
  • Spoilers, slash commands and long-press reactions on mobile

Roles, permissions and moderation

Give people exactly the access they need with a role hierarchy and per-channel overrides. Moderators can mute, kick and ban, and every action lands in the server's audit log.

  • Colored roles and per-server display names
  • Invite links with expiry and use limits

Disappearing messages, down to none at all

Set how long a channel or DM keeps its history, from seconds to years. Or pick live-only mode, where nothing is saved in the first place.

  • Per channel and per DM, visible to everyone in the conversation
  • Group DMs with typing and online status
Apps

On every screen you use.

Install hasht on your phone or desktop, or just open it in a browser. The apps connect to Hasht Cloud or any self-hosted server, and you can add more than one.

  • Push notifications with quiet hours and snooze
  • Share photos and files straight from your phone
  • Desktop tray with unread badges

Notifications you control

Per-channel levels, quiet hours, snooze, and an option to hide message content on your lock screen.

Scheduled messages and drafts

Write now, send later. Drafts follow you between devices so you can finish a thought anywhere.

Inbox for unreads

Your data

Run it on our servers or yours.

Hasht Cloud is the easy way in. If you'd rather keep everything on your own hardware, the same app runs anywhere Docker does.

Self-host in one command

A single binary serves the app and API, backed by PostgreSQL. Migrations run on startup, and a TURN relay for calls is included.

# one stack, one command docker compose -f docker-compose.prod.yml up -d

No ads, no data sales, no training

We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or train AI models on your messages. The apps contain no ad SDKs or third-party analytics.
